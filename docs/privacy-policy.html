Privacy Policy

Beauty Camera by Martand Tech Labs

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Beauty Camera is published by Martand Tech Labs. This policy describes how the app handles your data.

1. Your Photos and Videos Stay on Your Device

Every photo and video you take or edit is processed on your phone and stays there. Beauty Camera never uploads images to any server, has no accounts or logins, and uses no AI. Your favourite filters and history are stored only on your device.

2. Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve, measure and protect ads, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect and send to Google:

  • Your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • Basic device and app information, and how you interact with ads
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data for the ad SDK

This data is used for advertising, ad measurement and fraud prevention, and is sent encrypted. We do not receive it as personal data and do not sell it. Google’s use is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent message before ads can use your data. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Me → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s Settings (Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads).
  • Buying Beauty Camera Pro removes all ads.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: to take photos and record videos.
  • Microphone: only to record sound with videos.
  • Notifications (optional): for the daily look reminder, which you can turn off in the app.
  • Storage: on Android 10 and above, saved photos go to your gallery through MediaStore with no storage permission. On Android 8–9, they are saved to the app’s own storage.
  • Internet: only for ads and Google Play services (purchases and reviews). All editing works offline.

4. In-App Purchases

A one-time “Pro” purchase is available through Google Play Billing. The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not collect payment details.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform) – ads and consent, as described in section 2.
  • Google Play Billing – the optional Pro purchase.
  • Google Play In-App Review – lets you rate the app without leaving it.

We do not share your data with any other third parties.

6. Children’s Privacy

Beauty Camera is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
