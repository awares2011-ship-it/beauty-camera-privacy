Privacy Policy

Beauty Camera by Martand Tech Labs

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Beauty Camera is published by Martand Tech Labs. This policy describes how the app handles your data.

1. Data Collection

Beauty Camera does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. No analytics, crash reports or telemetry are sent.

2. Photos and Videos

All photos and videos you take or edit stay on your device. The app never uploads images to any server.

3. Camera and Storage Permissions

  • The app requests camera access to take photos and videos.
  • On Android 10 and above, saved photos go to your device's gallery via MediaStore – no storage permission is needed.
  • On Android 8–9, photos are saved to app-specific storage.

4. In-App Purchases

A one-time “Pro” purchase is available through Google Play Billing. The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not collect payment details.

5. Third-Party SDKs

The app includes:

  • Google Play Billing Library
  • Google Play In-App Review Library

These libraries communicate with Google Play Services on your device. No personal data is shared with other third parties.

6. Children’s Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
